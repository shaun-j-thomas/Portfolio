Team Ignition: Pioneer 10,000ft Rocket Airframe

Leading a 4-person engineering team to design, manufacture, assemble, and test the Pioneer launch vehicle airframe with strict adherence to Design for Manufacture (DFM) principles.

Leadership Role Airframe Lead (4 Engineers)
Target Apogee 10,000 ft
Manufacturing CNC Machining & Layups

Subsystem Integration & Technical Leadership

Technical Leadership & Team Management

Directed cross-functional collaboration between recovery, propulsion, and avionics sub-teams. Established physical subsystem interface limits, maintaining CAD version control and guiding the team from initial conceptual sketches to flight hardware.

Design for Manufacture (DFM) & CNC Machining

Oversaw the precision CNC machining of internal centering rings, coupler bulkheads, and fin tabs according to strict Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T) standards to guarantee smooth assembly alignment.

Composite Thermal & Aerodynamic Protection

Applied customized fiberglass composites onto the nose cone and airframe body tubes to protect sensitive electronics against aerodynamic friction heating during high-speed ascent.

Structural Hydrostatic & Pyrotechnic Validation

Validated structural integrity through hydrostatic pressure testing of composite tubes and conducted ground-based pyrotechnic ejection tests to confirm reliable separation of recovery bays under pressure.
